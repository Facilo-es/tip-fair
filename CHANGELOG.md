# Changelog

## 1.0.2 (2026-11-15)

- Records reply letter: when tips for the asked months are paid later, it says under which month they will appear, and it says "No tips were paid in these months" instead of showing only zeros.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-15)

- The paste file is now plain ASCII (accents and currency signs written as escapes), so copying it from any browser view keeps £, € and accented statement text intact.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-15)

First release.

- Import tips from a till export (Square, SumUp, Teya, Lightspeed, Clover, Zettle, Dojo) or type them in; cash tips in one row.
- Shares equally, by hours, by role points, or both, with optional group pots (kitchen and front of house).
- Exact to the penny with largest-remainder rounding.
- Deadline check for payouts.
- Private statements in 6 languages, with English always shown.
- Monthly records, keep-until dates and the record request letter (Great Britain).
- Tipping policy builder (Great Britain) and tips notice builder (Ireland). Northern Ireland mode has no legal text.
- Free for up to 5 people sharing in a period; Pro licence through Freemius.
